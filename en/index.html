Privacy Policy — Meu Ateliê

Last updated
2026-10-31

This Privacy Policy explains how information is used and protected in the Meu Ateliê: Moda e gestão app.

1. What information is used

Meu Ateliê allows you to enter information needed to organize and manage a fashion atelier, including:

Clients: names, telephone numbers, email addresses and notes.

Measurements and photographs: body measurements, images of garments and information needed for making and alterations.

Orders: descriptions of garments, production stages, fittings, deliveries and appointments.

Materials and services: materials used, suppliers, labor and costs.

Finances: quotes, prices, installments and recorded payments.

Atelier details: name, contact details, address, tax identification and logo, when provided.

The information entered depends on the features used. We recommend entering only the data needed to provide services.

When entering information about minors, the establishment must take appropriate precautions and observe applicable legal requirements.

The app keeps a record of acceptance of the Terms of Use on the device, including the accepted version and the date recorded. This information is not sent to the developer’s servers or included in business backups.

2. What this information is used for

The information entered is used to organize client service, track orders, manage materials and services, prepare quotes, record payments, manage appointments and generate documents.

Meu Ateliê does not process payments made by establishments’ clients, regardless of the payment method, such as Pix, cards, bank transfers, deposits or cash. Amounts recorded in the app are used exclusively for the atelier’s financial and administrative management.

The Ateliê Premium subscription is purchased through Google Play and is separate from payments recorded by the establishment.

The establishment using the app is responsible for the information it enters, how it uses that data in its activities and taking precautions to preserve its confidentiality and security, in accordance with applicable law.

3. Storage and sharing

Meu Ateliê allows you to carry out management activities without an internet connection. Information entered, including client, supplier and order details, measurements and photographs, is stored on the device itself.

The app does not synchronize business records with the developer’s servers. The developer therefore has no remote access to information entered on the device.

An internet connection is used to look up, purchase and validate Ateliê Premium subscriptions through Google Play, including automatic checks that do not require a purchase. These services may process information related to purchases and subscriptions, their operation and technical characteristics of the device. App updates may be obtained through Google Play, depending on device settings.

The app allows users to generate quotes, worksheets and backups. When users save or share these files, information may be stored and processed by the services and apps they choose.

Automatic backups are stored in the folder authorized by the user. We also recommend keeping manual backups, preferably in another secure location.

Meu Ateliê support answers questions about how the app works, receives suggestions, compliments and complaints, and handles requests concerning subscriptions and privacy. Information provided through this contact is used to analyze and respond to requests.

Meu Ateliê support and the developer do not request payments by Pix, bank transfer, deposit or other direct methods, nor do they ask for card numbers, bank account details, passwords, authentication codes or confidential financial information to provide assistance, resolve problems or unlock features.

Ateliê Premium, when available, is purchased exclusively through the official Google Play process, under the conditions and at the prices presented by the store itself.

Be wary of messages, calls or contacts requesting payments or confidential information in the name of Meu Ateliê. Do not make payments or provide this information through unofficial links, messages or channels. If in doubt, use the support contact provided in this Policy.

Support does not request remote access to your device. It also does not require complete client records, photographs or backups as a condition of providing assistance.

If additional information is needed to assess a request, only relevant details will be requested, such as a description of the problem and the app version.

4. Security and data retention

Meu Ateliê uses protection and storage mechanisms provided by the operating system, as well as checks intended to preserve the integrity of records.

Information entered is stored on the device used. We recommend taking appropriate steps to protect access to the device and files, such as enabling a screen lock, keeping the operating system up to date, avoiding apps from unknown sources and making regular backups.

The app allows backups to be password-protected. This protection applies to the selected backup files and does not mean that all data stored on the device is encrypted by the app.

Records remain stored for as long as they are kept in the app, subject to deletion operations and the need to preserve certain histories.

Loss, theft, damage or replacement of the device, clearing data, uninstalling the app, malware or unauthorized access by others may compromise the confidentiality and availability of stored information.

Meu Ateliê does not keep copies of business records on its own servers and therefore cannot remotely recover lost records. Recovery depends on having valid, accessible backups.

These precautions do not remove legal responsibilities relating to the operation and security of the app itself.

Information sent to support is retained only for as long as needed to handle requests and meet any legal obligations, keeping only records that are justifiably necessary.

5. Data deletion and user rights

The app provides features to view, update, archive and delete information, according to the nature of each record.

Deleting information in the app does not automatically remove previously exported files, backups, shared documents or copies held by other services.

Business information stored exclusively on the device cannot be accessed, modified or deleted remotely by the developer.

Data subjects may exercise the rights provided by applicable legislation, including requests for access, correction and deletion, subject to legal conditions.

For information entered by an atelier, requests should be directed to the establishment responsible. For information provided directly to Meu Ateliê support, requests may be sent to the contact listed at the end of this policy.

Support requests are handled manually. When needed to confirm identity and exercise rights, only relevant information will be requested, subject to applicable legal time limits. After a request is handled, unnecessary messages and attachments are deleted from copies under the responsible person’s control, subject to legal obligations and justified retention needs.

6. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app, its services or applicable legislation.

The date of the latest update will be shown in the document. Significant changes will be communicated appropriately.
